# The Autoimmune Problem
## Why are autoimmune diseases considered a silent epidemic?
Autoimmune diseases are highly prevalent, costly, and often overlooked. They affect a significant portion of the workforce and lead to escalating medical costs.
## How much of specialty drug spend is driven by autoimmune disease?
50% of specialty drug spend is driven by autoimmune disease, and this spend has grown 459% over the past decade.  
(Source: [Center for Biosimilars, 2023](https://www.centerforbiosimilars.com/view/contributor-the-us-biosimilar-market-outlook-for-2023))
## What percentage of workers suffer from autoimmune conditions?
15% of workers suffer from an autoimmune condition. Over the past 30 years, prevalence has more than doubled.  
(Source: [PubMed, 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32266792/))
## How much does autoimmune care cost on average?
For members with Rheumatoid Arthritis, the average annual cost is $34,000. Autoimmune patients are 4 times more expensive than the typical member.  
(Source: [ACR Abstracts, Total Cost of Care for Patients with Rheumatoid Arthritis](https://acrabstracts.org/abstract/total-cost-of-care-for-patients-with-rheumatoid-arthritis/))

# WellTheory Founding Story
## Why was WellTheory founded?
WellTheory was founded based on the personal health journey of its founder, who struggled with debilitating autoimmune symptoms at age 25. After years of being dismissed by doctors, she was diagnosed with a positive ANA, a hallmark sign of autoimmune disease. Through lifestyle and nutrition changes, she was able to reverse many symptoms and reclaim her health. This experience inspired her to build WellTheory so others would not have to go through the same long, difficult path.
## What personal experiences shaped the creation of WellTheory?
The founder experienced brain fog, gastrointestinal symptoms, fatigue, rashes, bloating, and tremors, and was bedridden at one point. Multiple specialists dismissed her concerns despite ongoing symptoms. After discovering the autoimmune connection, she embraced food as medicine, changed her lifestyle, and shared her story publicly. Her story went viral on TikTok, reaching 1.6 million viewers, showing how underserved the autoimmune community was. Beyond the founder, WellTheory was built by a team of individuals who have also been personally impacted by autoimmune disease.
## How many people in the U.S. are affected by autoimmune conditions?
There are an estimated 50 million Americans living with autoimmune conditions. Over the past 30 years, this number has more than doubled, increasing from 20 million to 50 million.
## How do autoimmune conditions affect women and minorities?
- 75–80% of autoimmune patients are women.  
- Some conditions are up to 16 times more common in women than men.  
- Black and Hispanic women are diagnosed with lupus at nearly 3 times the rate of non-Hispanic white women and experience a mortality rate 2–3 times higher.  
- Women’s symptoms are more likely to be minimized, and women remain underrepresented in medical research.
## What systemic issues in healthcare contribute to the autoimmune epidemic?
- Women’s health concerns are often dismissed or labeled as psychosomatic.  
- Studies show women are prescribed less pain medication than men after the same procedures, are less likely to be admitted with chest pain, and are more often prescribed antidepressants instead of painkillers.  
- Historically, women were excluded or underrepresented in medical research, limiting understanding of how sex and gender affect disease.  
## What is WellTheory’s mission?
WellTheory’s mission is to reverse the autoimmune epidemic by addressing the root causes of disease, providing evidence-based care, and supporting members with personalized coaching and community.
## Who backs WellTheory financially?
WellTheory announced $7.2 million in seed funding, led by Accel with participation from Box Group, Lux Capital, Scribble Ventures, Rock Health, and other notable health-tech investors and operators.

# WellTheory Care Model
## How does WellTheory’s support compare to traditional primary care?
The average primary care visit lasts 18 minutes, and most people only receive two visits per year. With WellTheory, members receive video visits every few weeks plus unlimited messaging with our Care Team who specializes in a holistic and integrative approach to autoimmunity.
## How does WellTheory support members between medical appointments?
WellTheory uses a collaborative care team model, with Registered Dietitians and Certified Health Coaches working alongside members' existing physicians and specialists to provide continuous support between medical visits.
## What is WellTheory’s holistic approach?
WellTheory helps members build sustainable habits across four key lifestyle areas:  
- Nutrition
- Stress
- Sleep
- Movement
These habits are integrated into everyday life to reduce symptoms and support long-term health.
## How does WellTheory view the healing process?
Healing is not linear. WellTheory takes a collaborative and adaptive approach, adjusting care as members’ needs evolve. The Care Team provides flexible support rather than prescriptive solutions, ensuring members are guided at every stage of their journey.
## What kind of ongoing support do members receive?
Members have access to 1:1 video sessions as well as unlimited messaging with our Care Team. Questions, concerns, or flare-ups between appointments can be addressed at any time.
## Does WellTheory offer advanced testing?
Yes. Members can access advanced, integrative testing to identify root causes of their symptoms. Remote ordering and at-home kits provide insights not typically available in standard doctor visits.
## How does WellTheory provide continuous care?
Members receive highly personalized nutrition and lifestyle support tailored to their root causes and evolving needs. This ensures care continues even when symptoms are sporadic.
## Who makes up WellTheory’s Care Team?
WellTheory’s Care Team is made up of licensed experts — including registered dietitians, health coaches, and care coordinators — who also live with autoimmune conditions. They are not traditional “-ologists” but peers who understand the lived experience of autoimmune disease. Members may also refer to them as practitioners or experts.
## What lifestyle factors does WellTheory focus on?
WellTheory supports members in four key areas of influence:  
1. Nutrition 
2. Sleep
3. Stress management
4. Movement
## What kind of expert support do members receive?
Members are supported by a Care Team of autoimmune experts, led by a registered dietitian. Support includes regular video calls and unlimited messaging.
## How is care personalized at WellTheory?
Care is dedicated and individualized. The Care Team works with each member to uncover the root causes of symptoms and create a whole-body approach addressing nutritional and lifestyle needs.
## Do members have access to a community?
Yes. Members can join a supportive community of others living with autoimmune conditions, with access to a resource library and opportunities for shared learning and encouragement.

# The 12-Month Healing Journey
## How long do members typically work with their Care Team?
The program lasts 12 months and is divided into two phases:  
- **Phase 1 (first 6 months):** Regular 30-minute live calls to uncover root causes, reduce symptoms, and build sustainable habits.  
- **Phase 2 (next 6 months):** Monthly 15-minute check-ins and ongoing support to maintain progress. 
This structure allows most members to experience symptom relief, develop health awareness, and gain tools to manage their autoimmune condition(s) long term.

# WellTheory FAQs
## Who is eligible for WellTheory?
WellTheory is free for members (18+) enrolled in a participating health plan through our partners. When creating your account, you will complete a brief clinical questionnaire to ensure the program is a good fit for your needs.
## Is there really no cost to join WellTheory? How?
Yes. WellTheory is covered through employers and health insurance plans. There is no cost to members, and there are no copays or hidden fees.
## How does WellTheory fit into my existing care and regimen?
WellTheory integrates with your current healthcare team. The focus is on managing symptoms through lifestyle adjustments in nutrition, stress, sleep, and movement. Progress is tracked using clinically-verified methods.
## Can I change my coach or Care Team member if it’s not a good fit?
Yes. If you do not feel a connection with your coach, WellTheory will work with you to match you with a better fit. While most members do not need to switch, finding the right coach is considered an important part of healing.
## Do I need a formal diagnosis to join WellTheory?
No. A formal diagnosis is not required. Many people living with autoimmune symptoms have not yet received an official diagnosis. WellTheory is designed to support both those with confirmed conditions and those experiencing symptoms that may be autoimmune-related.
## Do you work with individuals who share my diagnosis or symptoms?
Yes. WellTheory specializes in helping anyone with autoimmune disease or autoimmune-like symptoms on their healing journey. A formal diagnosis is not required, and we support members with over 100 autoimmune and inflammatory conditions.
## Can I pay for my membership with HSA or FSA dollars?
Yes. WellTheory accepts payment from Health Savings Accounts (HSAs) and Flexible Spending Accounts (FSAs) using debit or credit cards. In most cases, your HSA or FSA provider will request a Letter of Medical Necessity, which you will need to obtain from your healthcare provider.
## Can I pay out of pocket for WellTheory?
Yes. In addition to being covered through participating employers and health plans, WellTheory is also available as a direct-to-consumer service. Members who are not part of a partner plan can choose to pay out of pocket to access the program.
## Can you order labs or advanced testing for me?
Yes. WellTheory offers a range of optional advanced testing, including gut microbiome analysis, food sensitivities, hormone panels, and environmental toxins. Your Care Team will recommend testing based on your symptoms and health goals. Testing is not included in the membership fee.
## What happens after my program? Do I get to keep access?
Yes. After members complete their initial 12-month program, they keep access to the WellTheory app, including symptom trackers, journals, curriculum content, the online community, and special events. Members may also have the option to continue 1:1 coaching sessions.
